Our Mission

Saving a video you like shouldn't need an app install, an account, or a wall of ads. Fast Video Downloader Pro exists to do one thing well: turn a link into a downloadable file, in the highest quality available, in a few seconds.

How We Work

You paste a public video link, our servers fetch the available formats and qualities, and you choose the one you want. We don't store your videos or require sign-up — the file goes straight from our server to your device.
